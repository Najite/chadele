About Chadele

Integrated Business Advisory, HR Consulting & Talent Solutions

Headquartered in Lagos with strategic operations in Abuja, Chadele empowers forward-looking enterprises with end-to-end corporate and human capital solutions. We unite boardroom governance, statutory HR compliance, executive recruitment, and workforce training under a cohesive execution framework.

  • 1. Strategic Business Advisory
    Operating model redesign, organizational architecture, board advisory, and change management strategies that position your company for scalable market expansion.
  • 2. Full-Suite HR Consulting
    Airtight employee handbooks, 100% statutory Labour Act compliance audits, objective OKR performance systems, and Radford-calibrated total rewards design.
  • 3. Recruitment & Talent Solutions
    Targeted executive search, specialized technical sourcing, candidate scorecard assessments, and an unconditional 12-month candidate replacement warranty.
  • 4. Corporate Training & Capability Building
    Executive leadership accelerators, people management masterclasses, statutory compliance workshops, and measurable culture transformation programs.
